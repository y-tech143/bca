01 — A definition, sort of

Friendship isn't just a word.

It isn't measured in years known, or messages sent, or photos posted. It's what's left when none of that is happening — and you're still remembered anyway.

It's being there when it's inconvenient. Laughing for no good reason. Turning an ordinary Tuesday into something you'll bring up years later. It's the person who knows your order, your worries, and your worst jokes, and stays anyway.

It grows in the smallest, most unphotographed moments — a shared silence in a car, a text at 2am, a seat saved without being asked.
